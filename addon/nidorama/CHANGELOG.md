# Changelog

## 0.29.0
- Cut-out shapes in the model maker: a part set to Cut out removes its shape from the parts it overlaps, for half-round benches, corner baths, basins and more
- Fixed: the colour picker closed while you were still choosing a colour (in the model maker and in edit mode)
- Fixed: a scrollbar showed across the category chips in Furnish; the chips now scroll sideways by swipe or mouse wheel
- With thanks to ElCapitanDre: cut-out shapes were his idea, and he found both fixes (pull request #4)

## 0.28.0
- New phone in a home that already uses Nidorama? The first screen now looks for Home Assistant on your network and signs you in with one tap
- Once signed in, the homes already set up in your household appear by themselves
- Settings → Home Assistant has Find on this network
- If nothing is found, type the address on the first screen; it says when the Nidorama add-on is missing, and how to add it

## 0.27.0
- Make your own furniture: Furnish → Make your own opens the model maker, to build a piece from simple parts
- With thanks to ElCapitanDre: the model maker was his idea (issue #3), and he contributed its new shapes, dragging with snapping, mirror copy, four legs, whole-model size and height, the light option and copies that follow their model (pull request #4)
- Start from photos: save the guide file, give it and photos of your furniture to a chat assistant, and open the file it writes
- Paint anything already in your home, or change its shape: select it in edit mode → Paint it, or change its shape
- My models: what you make or change is kept for you, on this device and with the add-on for your Home Assistant user
- Search in Furnish: finds furniture, your models and community models as you type
- Community: share a model with everyone, and place models other people have shared
- Model maker: spheres, domes, cones (set each end's width, for lampshades) and wedges (choose which side slopes)
- Drag parts in the model maker: move them, or resize them by the dots on their faces; they snap to other parts and the floor
- Mirror copy, four legs under a part, and resizing the whole model at once
- Models can hang on a wall (placed at height), and can be a light
- Changing a model updates every copy placed in the home
- Save as my model on anything placed, and your models in Change how it looks
- Models placed in a home are shared with everyone in the household

## 0.26.0
- A test build of the model maker; everything in it is listed under 0.27.0

## 0.25.0
- Nido theme: the dashboard selector opens in place as your home's layers, floors top first, then widget dashboards and other homes
- Nido conceptual: a new plan style with no walls, just rooms as tiles, furniture as outlines and lit rooms washed in amber
- Every entity on the plan is a dot in both Nido styles, glowing while it is on
- Pick a colour for each kind of dot, and the highlight colour, in Look and feel → Nido colours
- Give a single entity its own dot colour in edit mode; tap and hold on the plan keep their actions

## 0.24.0
- Nido: a new theme and plan style, with your floors shown as layers
- Smooth moves between the whole site and its levels
- Paint, brick, sandstone, weatherboards and more on each side of a wall
- Paintable door and window trims
- Music Assistant one tap away in More
- Buttons can open Home Assistant, Music Assistant or Homey
- What's new is listed when an update is offered

## 0.23.1
- Units sit at the top of Settings: Metric or Imperial.
- Settings → Updates and backup starts with Check for updates automatically, On or Off.
- A waiting update shows as a green dot on the More button instead of a chip on the plan.

## 0.23.0
- Feet and inches: choose Metric or Imperial units when you first open the app, or later at the top of Settings. Homes keep their measurements either way.
- Trying the sample home? More → Start my own home shows the ways to begin yours.
- Edit homes and dashboards: each dashboard has one ⋯ button with all its actions, so the list fits on a phone.

## 0.22.2
- Fixed: "Make a plan from a paper sketch" on the welcome screen could not open the finished file.
- The sample file saved for a sketch now carries notes explaining every part, so a home file can be written from it more reliably.
- Home files may now contain comments and trailing commas, and furniture may be written in a short form (kind, position, turn).

## 0.22.1
- No GitHub sign-in in the app: requests and votes are made on GitHub, and updates need nothing.

## 0.22.0
- Roofs are dashboards of their own: draw roof pieces on the blueprint, drag their corners to
  size them, turn them with a handle, or cover the outside walls of any level in one tap.
- The whole site is made of your dashboards: each one stands on a floor (Ground up to Level 40)
  and is dragged and turned into place, catching on the floor below.
- Add level and Add roof under each home in the dashboard list, starting from the outside walls
  of another level.
- An eye beside every dashboard hides it from the list outside edit mode.
- The widget panel can show a web page or one of your Home Assistant dashboards, with a guide to
  the one Home Assistant setting it needs.
- A Support Nidorama button in Settings, where the build has a donation page.
