## You may, without asking

- Say truthfully that something **works with**, **is built on**, or **is a fork of** Nidorama.
  For example: "Tiles for Nidorama", "a fork of Nidorama with extra themes".
- Use the name to talk about the project: in articles, reviews, videos, talks and tutorials.
- Link to the project and show its icon next to that link, unaltered.
- Build and run the app for yourself, and share unmodified official builds, under its name.

## You may not

- Call a **modified** version "Nidorama", or give it a name or icon that could be confused with
  it. If you change the app and distribute it, give it its own name and icon, and say that it is
  based on Nidorama.
- Use the name or icon in a way that suggests the project's owner made, approved or supports
  your product, service or company.
- Use the name or icon on hardware, in a company or product name, in a domain name, or on
  merchandise, without written permission.
- Register the name, the icon, or anything confusingly close to them, as a trademark, business
  name, domain or app-store listing.

## If you fork

The AGPL lets you take the code, change it and share the result, as long as your version stays
under the same licence with its source available. Before you distribute your version:

1. Change the app's name and icon.
2. Change the app id (`app.nidorama.controller`) so it installs beside the original, not over it.
3. Keep the licence and the copyright notices.
