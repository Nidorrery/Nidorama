Privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026. This page covers the Nidorama app (Android, web and the Home Assistant add-on) and this website.

In short

Nidorama collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your homes, dashboards and settings stay on your devices and, if you use the add-on, on your own Home Assistant.

What the app connects to

  • Your home system (Home Assistant, or Homey), at the address you give it, to show and control your devices. This is usually on your own network.
  • GitHub, only if you leave update checks on (Settings → Updates): the app asks GitHub for the list of releases, and downloads an update when you choose to install it. You can switch this off.
  • GitHub, only if you use Requests (More → Requests): reading the requests log, and sending a request, vote or screenshot with your own GitHub token. What you send is published on GitHub under your GitHub account.
  • Poly Haven, only when you pick a 3D model from it, to download that model. You can switch the library off in Settings.

Nothing else. The app never sends your homes, devices, photos or settings anywhere you did not choose.

On your device

Homes, photos, 3D models, renders and settings are kept on the device. With the Home Assistant add-on, shared homes are kept in your Home Assistant's own storage. Backups are files you save yourself, wherever you choose.

The Android app asks for: network access (to reach your home system), notifications (only for alerts you create), and installing apps (only to install updates you choose).

This website

This site is plain pages with no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
