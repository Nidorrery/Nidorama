Your home, as a floor plan you can touch.

Nidorama is a dashboard for Home Assistant that looks like your home. Draw your rooms, drop in furniture, connect your devices, then tap the lamp to switch it and swipe to dim it. It runs on your own network: no accounts, no cloud, no tracking.

Get NidoramaSource code

What it does

A home builder

Draw walls by length, add doors, windows and floors, and pick from a furniture library. Several levels, several homes, and the whole site with roofs.

Your devices, where they really are

Connect furniture to your devices as you build. Tap, hold and swipe each do what you choose.

Widgets too

Weather, clocks, thermostats, music, cameras and calendars, on their own dashboards or beside the plan.

Make it yours

Plan styles from illustrated to realistic, themes with their own day and night looks.

The whole site

Every building, its roof and the garden, in one view. Tap a building to go inside.

Made for wall tablets

Use it as your tablet's home screen or in kiosk mode. Home Assistant's own app is one tap away.

Get it

Home Assistant add-on

Nidorama in your sidebar, and every screen in the house shares the same homes.

  1. Add the repository to Home Assistant, or paste https://github.com/Nidorrery/Nidorama in Settings → Add-ons → Add-on store → ⋮ → Repositories.
  2. Install Nidorama and open it from the sidebar.

Android

For phones and wall tablets.

  1. On the device, download the APK from the latest release.
  2. Open it and allow installs from your browser.
  3. Sign in to Home Assistant, or try the sample home.

Any browser

Once the add-on is installed, open it from Home Assistant on any computer, phone or tablet.
